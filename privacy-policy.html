Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

This policy explains how EventGiftHub handles personal information when you visit www.eventgifthub.com.ng, sign in, create an invitation or interact with an event. Contact the EventGiftHub team at eventgifthubng@gmail.com for privacy questions or requests.

Information used by the service

  • Account information: your email address and account identifier. If you choose Google sign-in, Google provides your basic profile information, including your name, email address and profile picture. EventGiftHub does not receive your Google password or request access to your Gmail messages, contacts or Google Drive files.
  • Invitations and event information: names, event type, dates, venues, photos and other details you choose to submit.
  • Guest interactions: information submitted for RSVPs, event passes, gifts, messages, Aso-ebi orders and photo uploads, where these features are used.
  • Payments and support: transaction references, payment status, gift amounts, payout details you provide and information included in support requests. Card entry takes place through Paystack's checkout.
  • Technical information: authentication session data, browser storage and service logs used to deliver the website, maintain sign-in and diagnose problems. Hosting and infrastructure providers may process IP addresses and browser information.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate accounts, connect hosts to their invitations, display event details, manage guest responses, support payments and event features, answer requests, prevent abuse and meet applicable obligations. Depending on the activity, processing is based on providing the service you request, your consent, legitimate interests in operating and protecting the service, or legal obligations.

Invitation visibility and sharing

Names, photos, dates, venues and other details included in a published invitation can be viewed by people who receive or obtain its link. Links can be forwarded. Messaging platforms may fetch and cache a preview containing the host's photo, names and event date. Do not publish information you want to keep private, and obtain appropriate permission before adding another person's information or photo. Hosts receive the guest responses and information needed to manage their events.

Service providers

EventGiftHub uses Google for optional Google sign-in, Supabase for authentication and data services, GitHub Pages for website hosting, and Paystack for payment processing. Cloudflare may provide website delivery and invitation-preview services. These providers process information needed for their functions. External services such as WhatsApp and map providers also apply their own privacy policies when you use their links or features. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or necessary to protect the service and its users.

Storage, security and retention

Service providers may process information outside Nigeria. Applicable data-protection requirements govern these transfers. We use access controls and encrypted connections to help protect information; no online service can guarantee complete security. Information is retained for as long as needed to provide the relevant service, handle disputes, protect against abuse and satisfy applicable recordkeeping obligations. Deleting an account does not necessarily remove records that must be retained for these purposes or copies already saved by invitation recipients and external platforms.

Your choices and requests

You can contact eventgifthubng@gmail.com to request access, correction or deletion of your account and personal information, or to raise a privacy concern. Please identify the account or invitation involved; we may need to verify your identity. Depending on applicable law, you may also request portability, object to or restrict processing, and withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. Withdrawal does not affect prior lawful processing. You can remove EventGiftHub's Google connection through your Google Account connections; this does not itself delete your EventGiftHub account.

You may raise a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

Children and photographs

Adults creating invitations involving children should have the authority and permissions needed to share their information and photographs. Contact us if you believe a child's information has been shared improperly.

Policy updates

Changes will be published on this page with an updated date. Contact us if you need clarification about how this policy applies to your information.
